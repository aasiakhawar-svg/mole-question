Question 2 (a). Potassium superoxide, KO₂, reacts with carbon dioxide to make potassium carbonate and oxygen.
4KO₂(s) + 2CO₂(g) → 2K₂CO₃(s) + 3O₂(g)
A sample of 13.0 g of potassium superoxide reacts with excess carbon dioxide. Calculate the volume of oxygen, measured at r.t.p., that is formed. Give your answer to three significant figures. [3]
